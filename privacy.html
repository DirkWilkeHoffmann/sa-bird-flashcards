Privacy policy

SA Birds · last updated 22 October 2026

SA Birds is a free flashcard app for learning the birds of Southern Africa, made by Dirk. It collects as little as possible, and nothing is sold, shared for advertising or used to track you.

What is kept on your phone

Your progress, settings, the photos you have seen and any birds you download for offline use are stored on your own device. You can delete downloads and saved photos from the app's Offline screen, or remove everything by deleting the app from your home screen.

Your location

If you choose Near me, the app asks your phone for your approximate location to pick the birds recorded nearby. This is worked out on your phone. Your location is never sent to us or anyone else.

What is saved online

  • Progress. For each bird: how often you got it right, wrong or needed a hint, and when. Also which birds you mixed up and which photos you hid. This is saved under a random private sync code so your progress can follow you to other devices.
  • Google sign-in (optional). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a Google account ID, and link your account to your sync code. We do not get your password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Flagged photos. When you flag a photo, we receive which photo and which bird it shows, so the photo can be reviewed. Nothing about you is included.

This information is stored with Supabase (our database provider, servers in London). Flag reports are emailed to the app's owner through Resend.

Photos, sounds and text

Bird photos come from iNaturalist, recordings from xeno-canto and iNaturalist, and descriptions from Wikipedia. Your phone fetches these directly from them, so they see the usual technical details of any web request (such as your IP address), under their own privacy policies. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

No ads, no analytics

The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

To have your online progress and account removed, email wilkehoffmann@gmail.com and it will be deleted. Signing out stops your progress being linked to your Google account on that device.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It doesn't knowingly collect more than described above from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: wilkehoffmann@gmail.com.
